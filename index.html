The story continues.

CoastalCraft is our Minecraft Bedrock realm. Every season brings another world, new places to explore, and more memories to carry forward.

This is our home for world maps: a place to find your way around the current season and look back at the worlds we have shared.

Before you explore

Find your way.

A few useful things to know about the CoastalCraft maps.

How do I use a world map?

Open a season, then drag to move around. Use the zoom controls or your mouse wheel to see more detail. On a touch screen, drag to pan and pinch to zoom.

Are these maps live?

These are rendered snapshots of the realm. New builds and terrain changes appear when a fresh map export is published.

Where are the Nether and End maps?

Season 9 has separate Overworld, Nether, and End maps, linked above. The available Season 10 export currently covers the Overworld.

Can I still see Season 9 markers?

Yes. Open the Season 9 Overworld marker map, or use View Markers on its Nether and End pages.
